2026 Convention Cleaning — SMS Notifications

Plant City Assembly Hall · June 12–14, 2026

This page describes the SMS notification program for cleaning department captains and supervisors serving at the 2026 Jehovah's Witnesses Regional Convention.

What is this program?

Cleaning captains and assistant overseers receive SMS text message notifications when a cleaning assignment is marked complete. This helps the cleaning department overseer coordinate in real time across all zones.

Who receives messages?

Only pre-assigned cleaning department staff whose phone numbers have been provided directly to the cleaning overseer (Clifton Davis). Numbers are not collected via this website.

How to opt in

Recipients are enrolled directly by the cleaning overseer as part of their volunteer assignment. To confirm enrollment, text START to (727) 295-4805.

Message frequency

Up to 5 messages per convention day (June 12–14, 2026 only). This is a one-time event program — no recurring messages after June 14, 2026.

Sample messages

✅ CLEANING COMPLETE
Friday, June 12 @ 8:45 PM ET
Anclote Congregation
Zone 2 South — Auditorium Right & Aud. Women's Restroom
Captain: Carson O'Donnell

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe immediately. You will receive no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help.

Message and data rates may apply. For support contact Clifton Davis at 727-278-7454.
