Information Collected

This app collects names and phone numbers of cleaning department captains and supervisors solely for the purpose of sending operational notifications during the 2026 Regional Convention (June 12–14, 2026).

How Information Is Used

Phone numbers are used only to send SMS notifications when a cleaning assignment is marked complete. Numbers are provided directly by the cleaning overseer and are not collected from end users via this application.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. Participants may receive up to 5 messages per convention day. Message and data rates may apply.

Non-Sharing of Information

Phone numbers and personal information are not sold, shared, or distributed to any third parties.
